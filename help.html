How can we help?

Quick answers about accounts, AI generation, files, billing, and privacy.

How do I create an account?+
Go to the sign-up page, enter your name, email, and password, and accept the Terms. You will land in your workspace immediately; a verification link is sent to your email to secure the account.
I forgot my password. What now?+
Open the password recovery page, enter your account email, and follow the reset link we send you. Links expire for security — request a new one if needed.
How does AI document generation work?+
Describe what you need in the Word Creator or Excel Creator prompt box. WORDY AI drafts the structure, formatting, tables, and formulas — then you refine and export.
Which file formats can I export?+
Native .docx for Word documents and .xlsx for Excel workbooks, ready to open in Microsoft Word, Excel, Google Docs, or Sheets.
Where are my files stored?+
Everything appears in your dashboard under Projects and My Files, where you can reopen, download, or delete them at any time.
How does billing work?+
Everyone is currently on the Free plan: 4 projects max and 2 AI files per day (resets daily). Paid plans are temporarily paused — when they launch, you will be able to manage or cancel anytime from Dashboard → Billing.
Can I get a refund?+
Yes — see the Refund Policy for eligibility, then contact support and we will process it.
Is my data private? Do you train on my documents?+
Your documents are yours. We apply strict zero-retention handling for document content. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
