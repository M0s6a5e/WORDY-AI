Refund & Cancellation Policy

WORDY AI allows eligible subscription users to request a refund when they decide the service is not suitable for their needs, provided the subscription has not been used to create more than 4 projects during the applicable subscription period.

Refund Eligibility — Projects Used 4 or fewer: eligible for refund review · More than 4: not eligible under the standard usage-based policy

Refunds may be available when:

✓

The subscription was purchased recently and is still within the applicable refund period defined by WORDY AI.

✓

The user has not used the subscription to create more than 4 projects.

✓

The account has not exceeded the stated usage threshold.

✓

The subscription has not been abused or used primarily for commercial exploitation before the refund request, where such restrictions are legally and commercially appropriate.

Refund request window: [X days]
Definition · What is considered a "project"?

A project refers to a document-generation task initiated by the user that results in a generated or completed Word, Excel, or other supported project output. This definition is configurable and should match how usage is measured in the application, since refund eligibility is evaluated against it.

Refund Request Process

Submit a requestContact WORDY AI support or use the refund request option in your account.
Account reviewWORDY AI checks your subscription status, purchase date, and project usage.
Eligibility checkThe platform determines whether your request satisfies the refund conditions above.
Refund processingIf approved, the refund is initiated through the applicable payment provider.
ConfirmationYou receive confirmation once the refund has been processed.
Refund Exclusions

A refund may not be available where, subject to applicable law:

  • More than 4 projects were created during the applicable subscription period
  • The applicable refund request period has expired
  • The account has violated the Terms of Service
  • The user has engaged in fraudulent or abusive activity
  • The refund request does not otherwise meet the stated eligibility criteria

These exclusions are not intended to limit any legally mandatory consumer rights that apply in your jurisdiction.

Payment Processing and Refunds

Refunds are processed through the payment provider used for the original transaction. WORDY AI may use different payment providers depending on your country or region — see the Payment Policy below for details. Provider-specific refund timelines depend on that provider's own processing schedule.
